## Overview

<!-- Introduce the oscilloscope-inspired approach to TSDB exploration -->
<!-- This entire section should remain concise -->

TSDB Explorer aims to bring the familiar oscilloscope experience to time series database exploration.\
Instead of visualizing analog signals, it intends to provide an intuitive interface for navigating, analyzing, and investigating metrics from any TSDB in real-time.

### Why "Oscilloscope-like"?

<!-- Explain the motivation behind the "Oscilloscope-like" concept -->

Traditional monitoring dashboards are great for overview, but when you need to dig deep into your metrics — troubleshooting anomalies, measuring precise time deltas,
or exploring data interactively — you may need something more hands-on.

TSDB Explorer's main goal is to provide the same level of control and precision you'd expect from lab equipment, adapted for time series data.
